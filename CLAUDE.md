## Repository Purpose

neurolift-ai-fusion is the NeuroLift AI Fusion platform — a full-stack Avatar-Aide-Advocate training platform backed by a Python AI simulation engine. AI Avatars with ADHD traits experience authentic life struggles in a Sims/RPG-style environment while AI Aides provide real-time coaching. After sufficient training they fuse into Advocates.
